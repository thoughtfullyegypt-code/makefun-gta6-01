SOLMAR — a sun-soaked open-world crime game in the browser. You are Marco "Mars" Delgado, a wheelman in debt in a coastal city of your own: steal cars and drive them through a living grid city, dodge traffic and pedestrians, outrun the police under a 5-star wanted system, and run five story jobs for Ray — from a first stolen car to the last job at the warehouse. On-foot combat, a HUD with a live minimap, synthesized engine and siren audio, and saves in the browser. Built with three.js (vendored) and plain static files — no build step, no network at runtime. Everything is original: its city, characters, story and art are all its own.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. WASD to move/drive, mouse to look, E to get in and out of cars, click to shoot on foot, Space for the handbrake, Esc to pause.
